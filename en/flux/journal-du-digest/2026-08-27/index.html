/index 27 August 2026

Forty-five boxes and a few nets

A raw list of fifty GitHub references is transformed into a digest of forty-five resources that have been verified, deduplicated and grouped by use case. The archives are given a visual identity, with titles centred on the date and a grid without white gaps. The homepage and admin panel finally refine their margins and borders.

Requests and discussions

  • Analyse fifty GitHub links to produce a coherent compilation, rather than a list of top picks with no hierarchy.
  • Correct truncated URLs, moved repositories and duplicates present in the original list.
  • Group the resources editorially according to what they enable users to do.
  • Add archive images and LinkedIn share visuals to the published Digest.
  • Identify and then remove the floating white border that appeared between certain archive cards and moved in line with the window width.
  • Remove ‘Pinboard’ from all archive titles, without deleting the term from summaries, historical texts or posts that actually refer to it.
  • Reserve the ‘Digest — date’ format for contemporary curations and allow historical archives to bear only their date.
  • Position the navigation bar at the top of the viewport, without any top margin or border.
  • Simplify the lines on the home page: no line beneath the manifesto, a simple frame around the search bar and the date, followed by a slightly thicker bottom line.

Decisions

  • Functional duplicates in the list have been merged. The fifty entries thus yield forty-five distinct destinations.
  • The public classification remains deliberately narrow: 18 resources fall under ‘Development’, 26 under ‘AI & Agents’ and one under ‘Design & Creation’.
  • The introduction to this edition organises the reading into four paths: learning by building, composing agents, training or utilising models, and then choosing specialised tools.
  • The date becomes the fixed element in archive titles. The word ‘Pinboard’ remains an indication of origin, never a systematic embellishment.
  • Cards within the same row must fill the entire available height so that the illustrated background remains continuous, regardless of the title’s length.
  • The search box uses shared borders: one pixel at the top, on the sides and between elements, and two black pixels at the bottom.
  • This journal publication is purely editorial and therefore does not alter the v1.22.9 version of the website.

Production

  • Verification of identities and correction of canonical GitHub addresses, particularly for Developer Roadmaps, MetaGPT, ComfyUI and iFixAi.
  • Removal of duplicate bonus items that repeated Coding Interview University, LangChain and n8n.
  • Drafting of French titles and summaries, addition of necessary tags and publication of the ‘Digest — 27 August 2026’ containing 45 links.
  • Generated rectangular and square visuals for use in the archives and on LinkedIn.
  • Stretched the archive card links to the height of their cells, which eliminates the responsive white bar visible beneath 24 July 2026.
  • Standardisation of 842 historical titles and adaptation of the archive generator to prevent the ‘Archive Pinboard’ suffix from reappearing.
  • Aligned the admin interface with the ‘Digest — date’ convention when creating a new curation.
  • Removal of the top margin in the admin panel on both desktop and mobile.
  • Removal of the top border beneath the homepage manifesto; common framing for the search bar and calendar; followed by reinforcement of the bottom border only.
  • Co-ordinated transition of the site and the admin panel from v1.22.4 to v1.22.9 in line with the five functional and visual updates.

Validation and deployment

  • Verification of links, usernames, categories and tag pages upon publication of the edition.
  • Successful Hugo build and targeted testing of the admin panel after each correction.
  • Check of the archives, the homepage and the administration panel in a real browser on both desktop and mobile.
  • No horizontal overflow confirmed at 830 and 375 pixels in width.
  • eecba5a04 successfully deployed using the GitHub Actions workflow.
  • v1.22.9 version verified in the public footer and in the admin panel, followed by an annotated tag applied to the commit that was actually deployed.
