Day 117 – The archives remember PrestaShop

Day 117 of the log: KEREDIT integrates the PrestaShop history into its workshop archives, with a legacy import, a dedicated admin screen and restored timeline controls.

Session timetable

  • Platform used: Codex
  • Primary environment: Codex App on Mac, KEREDIT repository dépôt local krampouz, VPS [private name]
  • Date covered: session opened on Saturday 18 July 2026 at midday and ended on Sunday 19 July in the morning
  • Observation window: 18 July 12:18:56 → 19 July 08:40:45 CEST, in two short bursts
  • Minimum duration observed: approximately 1h12+ (burst from 18 July 12:18 → 13:27, then 19 July 08:37 → 08:40)
  • Merged branches: PR #292, #293 and #294 into dev, version updated from 0.16.0 to 0.16.2
  • Main changes: integration of PrestaShop history, VPS import script, timeline handles
  • Minimum cumulative time since the start of the log: approximately 772h16+ (771h04+ as at 18 July, plus 1h12+ observed)

Quick read

Following the night of Production v2, the weekend takes on a different pace. The focus is no longer on development but on preserving the shop’s history: the years of orders placed via PrestaShop must not be lost behind the KEREDIT beta.

In three pull requests, KEREDIT gains a proper archive module. The Prisma schema accommodates the legacy history, an import service can read the PrestaShop archive, the admin panel displays a dedicated screen, and a deployment script runs the import all the way to the VPS. On Sunday morning, a final fix restores the circular handles to the timeline tiles.

A legacy import, not a quick fix

PR #292 establishes the complete structure. Three append-only migrations extend schema.prisma; the legacy-archive-import.ts service (471 lines) handles reading the archive with its unit tests, integration tests and set of fixtures; and legacy-archive-service.ts handles queries on the admin side.

The frontend follows the same pattern: adminArchives.ts client, entry point at AdminLayout, protected administration routes at /api/admin/archives, and an e2e test at admin-archives.spec.ts to validate the workflow. The whole package is delivered alongside updates to the changelog and BIBLIOTHEQUE.md.

Transfer the archive to the VPS

PR #293 fixes a detail that matters in production: the script deploy/import-prestashop-archive-vps.sh was not executable. Without the execution bit, the planned import onto the VPS remained merely an intention. The fix is minor but it is a prerequisite for the whole package.

The round handles on the timeline

On Sunday morning, PR #294 restores the circular handles on the archive timeline, which had been lost in the process. The fix also affects the documentation: docs/HOME.md, START_HERE_CODEX.md, the OKF index and the maintenance log are all updated as part of this, and the version is increased to 0.16.2.

A Sunday that counts double

This post covers both the end of Saturday and Sunday morning, as the PrestaShop batch had been merged on 18 July after the close of Day 116, dedicated to the forge. The pace is slower, but the direction is clear: the beta is not only building the future of the workshop, it is reclaiming its past.
