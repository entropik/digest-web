Day 102 – Linking the prize, the zine and the forge

Day 102 of the diary: rules for additional pages, production plans, stabilising the zine workflow and GOF corrections to the issues.

  • Main environment: VS Code WSL, Codex plugin, local repository

Quick read

Today brings together three strands that had been tending to go their separate ways: pricing, the zine product, and the GOF forge.

On the KEREDIT front, ee60947a adds rules for additional pages. The pricing is no longer just a basic matrix: it can now apply a surcharge per page, displays the fields in the product admin panel, keeps the sharing types aligned and covers the calculation with backend tests.

The commit ed6677e1 then sets out two action plans in plans/: removing the remnants of the local PDF renderer and stabilising the knip / QR dependencies. This isn’t visible in the product, but it’s important to ensure the repository doesn’t mix the historical local build with the GOF pipeline.

Today’s major KEREDIT update is cff0bd72: the zine order flow has been stabilised. The backend better distinguishes between production contracts, adds a dedicated GOF worker, connects the queues, adjusts WooCommerce, and covers the path with integration and worker tests.

Meanwhile, GOF is making progress on the notebook rendering:

  • 0e46658 displays spine and cover measurements in reports;
  • 58608c7 renders partial overflow of book pages;
  • 6717ae6 allows already-completed book jobs to be restarted;
  • 311d0eb restricts booklet overflow to facing pages.

The division of responsibilities is clear: KEREDIT maintains the accuracy of the project, price and order details, whilst GOF takes responsibility for the rendering and production artefacts.

Moving forward

The next point to monitor remains the contract between KEREDIT and GOF. The zines are starting to move in the right direction, but every addition of a pricing rule or book geometry rule must remain visible in the payload transmitted to the forge.
